Privacy Policy

At LS Tuition, accessible from our application platform, your privacy is extremely important to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by LS Tuition and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

We collect academic records, student details (such as names, roll numbers, and class designations), result marks, and notices uploaded through the platform panels to maintain accurate institutional logs.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used strictly for managing institutional operations, securely storing records on Firebase, updating class timetables, and communicating academic progress.

3. Firebase Cloud Security

All data processed within LS Tuition is stored securely utilizing Firebase database architecture. We implement robust security measures to protect your information against unauthorized access.

4. Contact & Developer Information

If you have any questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, you can contact developer Sreenand directly at 8289804072 or via support channels.
