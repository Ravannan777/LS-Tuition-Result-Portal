Help & Support Center

Welcome to the LS Tuition Help Center. Here you can find guidance on how to navigate the platform, manage student results, publish notices, update timetables, and connect directly with the developer for technical assistance.

1. Result Panel Guidance

Select the appropriate class, enter the correct student roll number and name, input respective subject marks, and click 'Upload Result' to sync securely with Firebase.

2. Notice Panel Usage

Provide a clear notice title followed by detailed descriptions in the text box. Click 'Publish Notice' to broadcast institutional updates instantly.

3. Timetable Management

Choose the target class from the dropdown menu, enter structured subject and time details, and click 'Update Timetable' to keep schedules current.

4. System & Cloud Sync

All records are stored securely on Firebase. Ensure an active internet connection during data submission to prevent synchronization errors.
